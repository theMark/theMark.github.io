MetPro

Designer and developer · 2016–2021

MetPro pairs clients with real nutrition coaches. For five years I was the designer and a developer on the platform, shaping the client and coach experiences and shipping them in code on web and mobile.

Context

MetPro started as one-on-one coaching for nutrition and fitness. The challenge was keeping that personal feel while serving far more clients than coaches could see one by one. The app had to feel like a coach in your pocket, not another calorie tracker.

Both sides

For clients, the mobile app: daily check-ins, meal logging, progress and messages with a coach who knows your name.

For coaches, tools that show who needs attention today, so one coach can carry a bigger roster without the relationship going cold.

Designing and shipping

This is where I learned to design and build at the same time. I shipped front end across the web app and the React Native apps on a Ruby on Rails backend. When the person who designs the screen also ships it, very little gets lost in between.
