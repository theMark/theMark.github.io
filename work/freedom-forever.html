Freedom Forever

Product designer · 2021–2026

Freedom Forever is a residential solar installer working across the United States. I designed the field app the crews work from, the customer app homeowners use to follow their project, and the internal tools operators use all day, including Lightspeed and an AI support agent.

Context

A solar install moves through dozens of steps over weeks: survey, permits, install, inspection, activation. It involves field crews, project managers and a homeowner who just signed a 25-year commitment. The tools holding it together were built for the office, but the work happens on driveways and rooftops.

Behind that, the business runs on back-office work like pricing, permits, commissions and scheduling, which was spread across spreadsheets and several systems.

The field app

The crew's view of the job: schedules, site details, documents and status updates, often used with gloves on and sun on the screen. I designed it for one hand and quick scanning, and we used Mixpanel to see where crews got stuck.

The customer app

A clear view of a long process for homeowners: where their project stands, what happens next and who to talk to. I designed and coded this one, working in Figma and Claude Code side by side, so a morning mock could be running on a phone by the afternoon.

Internal tools

Lightspeed, the pipeline that tracks a project from sale to activation.

Scheduling, matching crews, trucks and jobs across regions.

Commissions, which calculates and explains pay for the sales team.

An AI support agent and the framework behind it, built with the Claude Agent SDK and ElevenLabs for voice.

What I took from it

Operators spend eight hours a day in these tools, so every extra click gets multiplied by hundreds of people. And the field app and customer app turned out to be one product: every status a crew logs is a moment of reassurance, or worry, for a homeowner.
